Welcome to CitySnap India
A daily geography game — 5 rounds, one guess each.
1
You'll see a satellite + road map view of a city in India, zoomed in enough to see its shape and roads — but with no labels.
2
Type your best guess for the city name and hit Guess. You get one attempt per round.
3
Score is based on how close your guess is to the actual city — an exact match earns full points, and the further away your guess, the fewer points you get.
4
Points are multiplied in later rounds — rounds 3, 4, and 5 are worth more, so a great guess late in the game can turn everything around.
5
Play all 5 rounds and share your total score with friends!
